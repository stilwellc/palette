What to take homeQS–26

Four rules for quiet.

1Start here

Off by default.

A person turns a notification on. The product never does it for them.

2

Batch, then send.

One digest at an hour they chose beats nine separate pings.

3

Say it in the app.

If it can wait until they open the product, it waits.

4

Count every send.

Sends per person per day is a metric, and it has an owner.

On Monday

Turn off one notification and watch retention for 30 days.We did it 35 times. Retention went up two points.
